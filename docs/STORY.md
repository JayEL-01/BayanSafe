# BAYANSAFE: A Philippine Smart Community Disaster Intelligence and Preparedness and Response Game

## MAIN STORY

The Philippines is an archipelago frequently exposed to typhoons, floods, earthquakes, landslides, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions. These hazards can threaten communities, damage infrastructure, interrupt essential services, and force families to evacuate their homes.

The story begins in a fictional coastal barangay in Eastern Samar. The player is a young Filipino survivor who lives in a community where families depend on fishing, farming, and small local businesses. Life changes when an approaching typhoon threatens the barangay.

At first, the residents believed the storm would pass without causing major damage. However, heavy rainfall causes a nearby river to rise, low-lying roads become flooded, and several families are forced to leave their homes. During the emergency, the player helps residents prepare supplies, follow evacuation instructions, and reach a designated evacuation center.

While assisting the community, the player discovers a damaged emergency communication device containing an unusual signal. The signal appears to connect several disaster-prone locations across the Philippines.

As the player travels to help other communities, similar emergencies occur in different locations. Earthquakes damage buildings, landslides block roads, typhoons threaten coastal settlements, and volcanic activity forces residents to evacuate.

The player gradually discovers that the unusual signals originate from a machine created by Dr. Elias Voss. The machine was designed to control environmental forces, but it malfunctioned and began interfering with systems connected to disaster monitoring and communication.

Although the machine is fictional, the emergencies faced by the player reflect hazards that occur in the Philippines. The player must learn to distinguish reliable warnings from rumors, prepare emergency supplies, recognize natural warning signs, follow evacuation instructions, and coordinate with local responders.

Throughout the journey, the player must make decisions before, during, and after each disaster. These decisions affect the number of residents assisted, the resources available, and the condition of each community.

What begins as a mission to help fellow Filipinos survive emergencies eventually becomes a race to stop the machine before more communities are placed in danger.

Main educational objective: Teach players practical disaster preparedness and safety decisions using guidance from Philippine government agencies, especially the NDRRMC, Office of Civil Defense (OCD), PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, and local DRRM offices.

## PROLOGUE — THE FIRST WARNING

### START: TUTORIAL — The Day Everything Changed
- **Location:** Barangay San Isidro, Eastern Samar
- **Main Objective:** Explore community, interact with residents, collect basic items, learn controls. Prepare family emergency go bag.
- **Go Bag Contents:** Drinking water, ready-to-eat food, flashlight, batteries, first-aid kit, essential medicines, identification documents, and other necessities.
- **Learning Points:**
  - Check official weather updates from PAGASA and announcements from local authorities.
  - Identify the designated evacuation center and the safest available route.
  - Prepare emergency supplies before conditions worsen.
  - Help children, older adults, persons with disabilities, and residents who need assistance.
  - Report hazards to barangay officials instead of attempting dangerous rescues alone.
- **Decision:** Player initially wants to stay behind to protect belongings vs. follow evacuation instructions and prioritize human life over property.
- **Correct Decision:** Follow evacuation instructions; prioritize human life over property.
- **Story Development:** At the evacuation center, a damaged radio receives an unfamiliar transmission with coordinates pointing toward another community.
- **Educational Basis:** PAGASA tropical cyclone warnings, storm-surge guidance, LGU/local DRRM preparedness materials.

### Level 1 — Typhoon: The First Trial
- **Location:** Neighboring coastal settlement
- **Main Objective:** Prepare the community for the typhoon, follow evacuation instructions, and help residents reach safety.
- **Scenario:** Strong winds threaten houses, tree branches fall across roads, residents struggle to secure belongings. Player distributes preparedness info, checks supplies, guides residents to evacuation center.
- **Decision:** Convince residents whose houses are not strong enough that danger depends on local conditions, building construction, and warnings. Avoid exposed areas, flying debris, damaged structures, and unnecessary travel. Stay in designated safe location instead of attempting risky rescues.
- **Aftermath:** Help report damaged houses, locate missing residents, identify families needing assistance.
- **Story Development:** Radio recovered from damaged communications station contains the same unusual signal. Player suspects disasters are connected.
- **Lesson Learned:** Monitor PAGASA updates, prepare early, follow evacuation instructions, and avoid unnecessary exposure during dangerous weather. PAGASA Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals describe wind threats and warning lead times; flooding and storm surge must also be considered.

### Level 2 — Flash Flood: The Rushing Waters
- **Location:** Settlement near a rising stream
- **Main Objective:** Recognize rapidly rising water, guide residents toward safer ground, and avoid dangerous flood crossings.
- **Scenario:** Heavy rainfall causes stream to rise quickly. Low-lying paths flood, residents stranded on wrong side of flooded road.
- **Decision:** Stop villagers attempting to cross floodwater to retrieve belongings; report blocked route to responders.
- **Player Actions:** Monitor rainfall/flood warnings; move residents to higher ground; avoid walking/swimming/driving through floodwater; protect drinking water/food from contamination; use alternative route only when identified as safe.
- **Story Development:** Damaged electronic component found near flooded communications station carrying the same symbol. The strange signal was detected before flooding began.
- **Lesson Learned:** Do not cross dangerous floodwater to retrieve property. Evacuate before routes become impassable and follow official warnings.

### Level 3 — Severe Flooding: The Sinking Settlement
- **Location:** Low-lying settlement with overflowing waterways
- **Main Objective:** Help residents evacuate, prioritize urgent needs, and preserve essential supplies when safe.
- **Scenario:** Streets submerged, families trapped in homes, water levels continuing to rise.
- **Player Actions:** Guide people to designated evacuation areas, prioritize vulnerable residents, avoid flooded routes. Decide which items are essential; discard optional belongings that delay movement to safety. At evacuation center, organize supplies, record needs, account for evacuees.
- **Story Development:** Damaged terminal reveals coordinates pointing toward a mountainous region: North West Plateau.
- **Lesson Learned:** When flooding persists and warning calls for evacuation, follow instructions immediately. Avoid flooded roads and report urgent needs to local responders. PAGASA flood-warning levels: monitoring, flood alerts, flood warnings, severe flood warnings.

## CHAPTER 1 — NORTH WEST PLATEAU
**Theme:** The Ground Beneath Us

### Level 1 — Earthquake: The Shattered Ground
- **Location:** Mountain settlement
- **Main Objective:** Survive the earthquake, assist residents, and investigate damaged communications station.
- **Scenario:** Strong earthquake strikes. Buildings shake, objects fall, cracks appear along roads.
- **Player Action:** Perform Drop, Cover, and Hold On during shaking, protecting head/neck away from windows/heavy objects. Move away from damaged buildings toward safer open area.
- **Decision:** Discourage residents from re-entering damaged structures to retrieve belongings before buildings are declared safe.
- **Aftermath:** Report damaged roads, identify residents needing medical help, assist responders with unaccounted-for persons.
- **Story Development:** Inside damaged communications station, player finds a map displaying unusual signals beneath the plateau recorded shortly before the earthquake.
- **Lesson Learned:** During strong shaking: Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Afterward: prepare for aftershocks, avoid damaged structures, follow official instructions.

### Level 2 — Landslide: The Collapsing Mountain
- **Location:** Hillside roads between barangays
- **Main Objective:** Help stranded residents, avoid unstable slopes, and reach a safer route toward the next settlement.
- **Scenario:** Prolonged rainfall weakens slopes. Rocks, mud, fallen trees block roads.
- **Decision:** Stranded residents want to cross landslide debris (shortest path). Player must identify safer alternatives, report unstable slopes, follow responder-approved routes.
- **Warning Signs:** Notice cracks in ground and fresh rockfalls; report warning signs and wait for instructions rather than approaching slope.
- **Story Development:** Damaged monitoring station contains records of unusual signals detected before earthquake and landslide.
- **Lesson Learned:** Earthquakes weaken slopes; intense rainfall triggers landslides. Avoid unstable slopes, report warning signs, never attempt to cross landslide debris without authorization.

### Level 3 — The Calm Before the Wave
- **Location:** Plateau settlement during clear weather
- **Main Objective:** Help the community recover, restore communication, and prepare for possible new hazards.
- **Scenario:** Weather clears, ground becomes quiet. Community begins repairing homes. Player checks residents, distributes supplies, helps restore comms equipment.
- **Warning:** Inconsistent readings at coastal monitoring station; radio operator reports network interruption.
- **Decision:** Some residents think emergency is over. Player explains recovery must pair with preparedness and vigilance. Update list of residents needing assistance.
- **Story Development:** Communications station receives tsunami warning for connected coastal community. The calm was misleading.
- **Lesson Learned:** Continue monitoring official information after disaster, support safe recovery, remain prepared for additional hazards. Recovery activities must not override evacuation instructions.

### Level 4 — Tsunami: The Wave of Destruction
- **Location:** Coastal community below plateau
- **Main Objective:** Evacuate threatened coastal residents, reach higher ground, and recover information from damaged monitoring facility when safe.
- **Scenario:** Strong shaking felt, sea retreats from shoreline.
- **Natural Warning Signs:** Strong ground shaking, unusual sea-level change (retreat/rise), roaring sound from the sea.
- **Decision:** Residents gather along shoreline to observe water. Player must immediately warn them to move away to higher ground or evacuation area. Prioritize vulnerable residents, avoid low-lying coastal routes.
- **Aftermath:** Wait for official instructions/all-clear before returning.
- **Story Development:** Damaged research facility reveals recording about a machine capable of manipulating environmental systems.
- **Lesson Learned:** When natural signs indicate local tsunami, move to higher ground immediately. Do not wait at shoreline or return until authorities give all-clear.

## CHAPTER 2 — CENTRAL TOWN
**Theme:** When Civilization Falls

### Level 1 — Flash Fire: Flames in the Streets
- **Location:** Commercial urban area
- **Main Objective:** Evacuate residents, avoid fire hazards, and recover information from damaged facility when safe.
- **Scenario:** Fire breaks out in commercial area; smoke spreads between buildings, escape routes cut off.
- **Player Actions:** Raise alarm, alert residents, guide people to safe exit/assembly area.
- **Decision:** Residents want to re-enter to retrieve phones/belongings. Discourage re-entry. Avoid smoke-filled rooms, burning structures, damaged electrical gear. Report trapped people to emergency responders rather than attempting unsafe rescue without training/equipment.
- **Story Development:** Scorched electronic component recovered bearing same symbol; signals affect urban infrastructure.
- **Lesson Learned:** Raise alarm, evacuate using safe route, contact emergency services, never re-enter burning building until declared safe (Bureau of Fire Protection guidance).

### Level 2 — Earthquake: The City in Pieces
- **Location:** Urban streets and health facility
- **Main Objective:** Protect residents during earthquake, assist responders afterward, and recover communications map.
- **Scenario:** Strong earthquake strikes urban center. Buildings shake, objects fall, roads damaged.
- **Player Actions:** Drop, Cover, Hold On until shaking stops. Guide residents away from damaged buildings to open areas.
- **Decision & Rumor Control:** Rumors circulate on social media claiming another earthquake will occur at an exact time. Player must verify information through official sources (PHIVOLCS) rather than forwarding unconfirmed claims.
- **Health Facility:** Direct residents to authorized assistance points without interfering with medical staff.
- **Story Development:** Damaged map indicates communications facilities linked to a central location beyond Central Town.
- **Lesson Learned:** Practice earthquake safety, prepare for aftershocks, avoid damaged buildings, rely on official information rather than unverified earthquake predictions.

### Level 3 — Typhoon: The City's Last Shelter
- **Location:** Weakened urban center
- **Main Objective:** Prepare evacuation centers, protect essential supplies, and guide residents away from unsafe buildings.
- **Scenario:** Approaching tropical cyclone threatens already earthquake-weakened structures.
- **Player Actions:** Help responders identify residents needing evacuation aid, direct families to designated safe shelters (avoid earthquake-weakened structures). Ensure supplies remain accessible.
- **Aftermath:** Debris blocks major city drainage route, creating massive flood risk.
- **Lesson Learned:** Monitor official typhoon warnings, follow evacuation instructions, avoid using damaged structures as shelters without proper assessment. Assess wind threats and local flooding risks separately.

### Level 4 — Flash Flood: The Streets Become Rivers
- **Location:** Flooded city streets
- **Main Objective:** Help residents move to safer ground, avoid dangerous water crossings, and restore emergency communication.
- **Scenario:** Heavy rainfall + blocked drainage causes rapid urban flooding. Streets turn into rivers.
- **Player Actions:** Guide residents to higher ground; avoid walking/driving through flooded roads. Stop residents attempting dangerous crossings to retrieve belongings.
- **Hazards:** Treat damaged electrical equipment and submerged outlets as life-threatening hazards; report to qualified personnel.
- **Story Development:** Restored communications receive radio message from the harbor reporting coastal emergency.
- **Lesson Learned:** Move to safer areas before access is cut off, avoid floodwater, report electrical hazards (PAGASA flood guidance).

## CHAPTER 3 — HARBOR
**Theme:** Trapped Between Land and Sea

### Level 1 — Flash Flood: The Broken Waterways
- **Location:** Harbor docks and coastal access roads
- **Main Objective:** Help harbor workers reach safety, protect essential supplies, and identify safe evacuation routes.
- **Scenario:** Heavy rainfall floods dock access road, stranding workers.
- **Decision:** Workers want to retrieve supplies from warehouse near water. Player chooses to protect human life, leaves supplies behind, reports inaccessible warehouse, identifies alternative routes.
- **Story Development:** Emergency radio receives warning about approaching typhoon; loss of roads and comms complicates response.
- **Lesson Learned:** Evacuate before routes become impassable, avoid flooded roads/waterways, report hazards instead of attempting dangerous rescues.

### Level 2 — Typhoon: The Storm at Sea
- **Location:** Harbor shoreline and fishing docks
- **Main Objective:** Prepare harbor community for typhoon and move residents away from exposed coastal areas.
- **Scenario:** Approaching tropical cyclone brings strong winds threatening boats, warehouses, houses.
- **Decision:** Fishing families want to go out to sea to secure/recover vessels. Player convinces them that going out during dangerous conditions risks lives.
- **Story Development:** Storm winds subside, but water levels begin rising rapidly in low-lying districts.
- **Lesson Learned:** Follow PAGASA advisories, prepare early, do not risk lives to protect boats or property during dangerous weather.

### Level 3 — Severe Flooding: The Harbor Underwater
- **Location:** Submerged harbor districts
- **Main Objective:** Evacuate flooded districts, organize emergency supplies, and recover harbor transmission when safe.
- **Scenario:** Continuous rainfall submerges lower districts, damaging warehouses, stranding families.
- **Decision:** Convince families attempting to stay for belongings to evacuate carrying only essential items safely.
- **Aftermath:** Organize drinking water, food, first-aid, comms equipment at shelter. Return with authorized responders to inspect communications station.
- **Story Development:** Transmission recovered linking unusual signals to a research facility in North East Plains.
- **Lesson Learned:** When severe flooding persists, follow evacuation instructions; do not return until authorities permit (PAGASA escalating flood warnings).

### Level 4 — Flash Fire and Tsunami: The Final Departure
- **Location:** Fuel-storage area and harbor coastline
- **Main Objective:** Evacuate residents from fire and possible tsunami threat, then reach safe departure point.
- **Scenario:** Flash fire breaks out near fuel storage, threatening buildings. While evacuating, a strong earthquake is felt, followed by reports of unusual sea-level movement.
- **Decision (Compound Hazard):** Escape fire without approaching shoreline; recognize tsunami threat; direct evacuees to higher ground away from both fire and coast. Do not hesitate to observe the sea.
- **Story Development:** Obtain coordinates of North East Plains facility; harbor suffered extensive damage.
- **Lesson Learned:** During fire, evacuate and do not re-enter. If earthquake/unusual sea-level change suggests tsunami, move away from shore immediately to highest available ground.

## CHAPTER 4 — NORTH EAST PLAINS
**Theme:** The Truth Beneath the Surface

### Level 1 — Typhoon: The Ruined Farmlands
- **Location:** Agricultural plains and farming villages
- **Main Objective:** Protect farming community, prepare for typhoon, preserve essential supplies when safe.
- **Scenario:** Tropical cyclone approaches plains, threatening farms and storage buildings.
- **Decision:** Farmers want to stay behind to protect crops and livestock. Player explains warnings and assists evacuation to safe areas. Prioritize human safety over property.
- **Story Development:** Farmer reports unusual ground movement near drainage channel where strange signals were detected before storm.
- **Lesson Learned:** Follow official typhoon warnings, prepare early, prioritize human safety over crops, livestock, or property.

### Level 2 — Flash Flood Escalating to Severe Flooding
- **Location:** Farmland streams and drainage channels
- **Main Objective:** Help residents evacuate as flooding worsens and report damaged drainage systems.
- **Scenario:** Streams rise rapidly, spreading into farmland paths and threatening isolated homes.
- **Decision:** Discourage residents from entering flood zone to recover tools/food. Move to higher ground; report damaged drainage and tampered monitoring equipment.
- **Story Development:** Evidence confirms strange signals are actively tampering with hazard monitoring infrastructure.
- **Lesson Learned:** Flash flooding develops rapidly; persistent flooding cuts off roads. Follow evacuation instructions, avoid floodwater, report infrastructure damage.

### Level 3 — Landslide: The Road to the Facility
- **Location:** Mountain road to remote research facility
- **Main Objective:** Help stranded residents, avoid unstable slopes, and reach research facility safely.
- **Scenario:** Prolonged rainfall weakens slopes; landslide blocks main road. People stranded near route.
- **Decision:** Resident insists on crossing debris to retrieve important research facility documents. Player explains additional collapse can occur; human rescue takes priority over documents. Follow authorized route; report slope cracks.
- **Story Development:** Records at facility identify Dr. Elias Voss as creator of the machine, who attempted to control environmental forces but lost control.
- **Lesson Learned:** Avoid landslide debris and unstable slopes after prolonged rainfall. Report hazards and follow responder-identified safe routes.

### Level 4 — Earthquake and Volcanic Eruption: The Sleeping Giant
- **Location:** Plains near volcanic region
- **Main Objective:** Survive earthquake, follow volcanic evacuation instructions, reach emergency station.
- **Scenario:** Strong earthquake damages structures near volcanic zone (Drop, Cover, Hold On). Ash begins rising from distant volcano; PHIVOLCS reports increased volcanic activity.
- **Player Actions:** Help residents monitor PHIVOLCS bulletins; enforce danger zones (no staying to observe eruption); protect against falling ash (protective measures, avoid unnecessary travel); avoid river channels threatened by lahars.
- **Story Development:** Emergency station recording reveals the machine's central facility is hidden in Volcano Valley.
- **Lesson Learned:** Follow PHIVOLCS bulletins and evacuation orders. Avoid volcanic danger zones, protect against ash exposure, remain alert to lahar hazards in river channels during heavy rain.

## CHAPTER 5 — VOLCANO VALLEY
**Theme:** The Last Chance to Save the Community

### Level 1 — Earthquake and Landslide: The Broken Approach
- **Location:** Steep mountain valley approach
- **Main Objective:** Navigate damaged landscape, help survivors reach safety, find safe route to facility.
- **Scenario:** Powerful earthquake shakes mountains; rocks fall, road collapses.
- **Decision:** Landslide blocks road. Player chooses longer safe alternative route rather than shorter path beneath unstable slope. Reports blocked road, guides survivors to safe area.
- **Story Development:** Discovers service entrance to abandoned research complex beneath the mountain.
- **Lesson Learned:** Earthquakes weaken slopes and structures. Avoid unstable terrain, watch for aftershocks, report hazards instead of attempting solo rescues.

### Level 2 — Volcanic Eruption: The Unexpected Emergency
- **Location:** Valley settlement and complex perimeter
- **Main Objective:** Recognize volcanic danger, follow evacuation instructions, protect against ash exposure, obtain control key.
- **Scenario:** Valley goes quiet, then monitoring station reports surging volcanic activity and heavy ashfall.
- **Player Actions:** Check official PHIVOLCS updates, enforce evacuation, prevent residents from staying to protect belongings. Limit ash exposure. Retrieve control key along authorized route into complex.
- **Story Development:** Records show the World Engine has been transmitting signals to every disaster-struck region in the game.
- **Lesson Learned:** Monitor PHIVOLCS bulletins, follow evacuation orders, avoid restricted areas, take appropriate precautions against volcanic ash.

### Level 3 — Flash Fire: The Burning Facility
- **Location:** Inner research complex
- **Main Objective:** Evacuate facility personnel, avoid fire hazards, reach main control room safely.
- **Scenario:** Damaged electrical systems and overheated machinery trigger fire. Smoke fills corridors; main exit blocked.
- **Decision:** Raise alarm, guide personnel to alternative exit. A researcher is missing in a burning room: player reports last known location to response team rather than entering burning room without training/gear.
- **Story Development:** Recover Dr. Voss's final research log: Voss increased machine power beyond limits causing instability; emergency shutdown controls are located in central control room.
- **Lesson Learned:** Raise alarm, evacuate through safe route, do not re-enter burning building, leave dangerous rescues to trained responders (Bureau of Fire Protection).

### Level 4 — Typhoon and Severe Flooding: The Final Disaster
- **Location:** Complex lower levels and exterior valley
- **Main Objective:** Protect remaining personnel, follow weather warnings, evacuate threatened areas, reach central controls safely.
- **Scenario:** Powerful tropical cyclone strikes valley. Flash flooding escalates into severe flooding inside lower sections; submerged electrical equipment creates deadly hazards.
- **Decision:** Persuade workers not to retrieve optional equipment from lower floors due to electrical flood risks. Follow remaining safe route to control room.
- **Story Development:** Every prior community decision, preserved resource, and assisted resident now impacts final status.
- **Lesson Learned:** Monitor PAGASA warnings, evacuate when instructed, avoid floodwater and electrical hazards, prioritize human safety over equipment or property.

## THE FINAL MISSION — SHUT DOWN THE MACHINE
- **Location:** Central Control Room
- **Scenario:** The player confronts the unstable World Engine. Warning alarms sound, global disaster map glows.
- **Confrontation:** Dr. Elias Voss appears via damaged comms, claiming the machine can still be repaired and controlled. Player must decide whether to attempt controlled shutdown or risk catastrophe.
- **Testing All Accumulated Lessons:**
  - Verify information through reliable sources.
  - Communicate with emergency responders.
  - Follow evacuation instructions and account for people reaching safety.
  - Preserve essential supplies only when safe.
  - Avoid dangerous routes and unauthorized rescue attempts.
  - Prioritize human life over property and optional objectives.
  - Send final status report through facility comms.

## THE THREE ENDINGS

### ENDING 1 — COMPLETE VICTORY: A COMMUNITY RESTORED
- **Conditions:** Player followed official safety guidance throughout the game, assisted vulnerable residents, preserved essential resources, and successfully executed controlled shutdown.
- **Narrative:** World Engine disabled without catastrophic failure. Signals vanish, emergency communications normalize. Local responders conduct organized recovery; families reunite at evacuation centers. Player returns to Eastern Samar; community participates in drills, go-bag checks, and follows warnings.
- **Final Message:** *“Survival begins with preparation, knowledge, and cooperation. Every informed decision can help protect a life.”*

### ENDING 2 — PARTIAL VICTORY: A COSTLY RECOVERY
- **Conditions:** Machine shut down, but supplies were lost, some evacuation needs overlooked, or decisions delayed assistance.
- **Narrative:** Machine stopped, but communities suffered greater damage due to delayed evacuations and lost resources. Recovery will take long; ongoing relief needed. Player returns home determined to improve local preparedness and coordination.
- **Final Message:** *“Survival does not end when the danger passes. Recovery requires cooperation, responsibility, and learning from every mistake.”*

### ENDING 3 — TOTAL FAILURE: WARNINGS IGNORED
- **Conditions:** Player repeatedly ignored official warnings, entered unsafe areas, failed to coordinate evacuations, wasted resources, or mishandled shutdown.
- **Narrative:** Shutdown fails; World Engine remains active. Comms go dark; contact lost with communities. Operations collapsed due to lost supplies and equipment. Final status report flickers out.
- **Final Message:** *“Warnings are meant to protect lives. When reliable information is ignored and safety is sacrificed, the consequences can reach an entire community.”*
- **Educational Note:** Endings are fictional game outcomes; real disaster outcomes depend on hazard severity, community preparedness, resources, and responder coordination.
